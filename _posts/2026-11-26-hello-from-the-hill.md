There's a particular kind of forgetting that only shows up in hindsight. You live through something that feels enormous, a move, a hard year, a conversation that changed your mind, and you're sure you'll remember every detail. Then a few years pass and all that's left is a feeling and a couple of blurry snapshots.

This blog is my small act of resistance against that.

<!--more-->

## What you'll find here

Stories, mostly. Moments from my life that taught me something, or made me laugh, or still make me wince a little. Some will be about big transitions. Others will be about tiny things, like a walk that cleared my head or a sentence someone said that stuck.

I'm not trying to give advice or build a brand. I just want a record that's honest and readable, something I can come back to and something others might enjoy stumbling across.

## How it gets written

Here's the slightly unusual part. Most posts start as a conversation with an AI. I talk through what happened, the messy, out-of-order version, and it helps me find the shape of the story: where it begins, what it's really about, which details matter.

> The memories and opinions are mine. The structure and polish are a collaboration.

I like this arrangement. It lowers the barrier between *having* a story and *telling* it, which is exactly where most of my stories used to die.

## Why "Fool on the Hill"

Partly a nod to a song I love. Partly because it fits: the view from a hill is a good place to look back on where you've been, and being willing to look a bit foolish is the price of writing honestly about yourself.

So, welcome. Pull up a rock. More stories soon.
