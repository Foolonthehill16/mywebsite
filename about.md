Hi, and welcome. This is a small corner of the internet where I keep the stories from my life that feel worth holding onto: the turning points, the quiet lessons, the funny detours, and the things I'd like to remember ten years from now.

Most posts begin as a conversation. I talk through an experience, and an AI writing partner helps me shape it into something readable. The memories and opinions are mine; the polish is a collaboration.

If something here resonates with you, I'd love to hear about it.
